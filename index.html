01 / The brief

Do not search the Monster.
Build the ladder.

The group has roughly 8 × 1053 elements. Brute force is the wrong game. The hunt follows compressed structure: local carriers, thermodynamic gaps, boundary algebras, rank-24 discrimination, orbifolds, defects and held-out detector predictions.

Parallelize the searches. Serialize the promotions. A failed trail—with its assumptions intact—is a trophy, not a loss.

    06 / Rules of the hunt

    Bring back evidence,
    not ectoplasm.

    01

    Name the object

    Hamiltonian, algebra, detector, theorem or material—not an atmosphere of resemblance.

    02

    Freeze the rival

    State the closest conventional or smaller-algebra explanation before opening the result.

    03

    Show the scar

    Return code, proof, residuals, convergence axes, calibration and source revision.

    04

    Mark the grave

    If it fails, record scope, assumptions and the minimum datum that could reopen it.
